# Exam Information

**Date:** 2 June 2026  
**Format:** Group Presentations / Posters  
**Note:** The exam takes place on the last practical day (Day 4). The morning session covers AlphaFold & Protein Structure; presentations are in the afternoon.

## Overview

The final assessment consists of group presentations where you apply the techniques learned during the course to a drug discovery problem of your choice.

## Requirements

- **Group size:** 2–4 students
- **Duration:** 15 minutes presentation + 5 minutes Q&A
- **Format:** Slide presentation or poster (your choice)

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Scientific Content | 30% | Correct understanding and application of AI/ML methods |
| Methodology | 25% | Appropriate choice and use of computational methods |
| Results & Interpretation | 25% | Clear presentation and critical discussion of results |
| Presentation Quality | 20% | Structure, clarity, visual quality, teamwork |

## Suggested Project Topics

1. **QSAR Model for a Novel Target** — Build a predictive model for a target not covered in class
2. **GNN vs. Fingerprint Comparison** — Systematic comparison on multiple datasets
3. **AlphaFold Structure Analysis** — Analyze predicted structures for a protein family
4. **Drug Repurposing with ML** — Use ML to predict new uses for existing drugs
5. **ADMET Prediction** — Build models for absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, or toxicity
6. **Molecular Generation** — Explore generative models for de novo drug design

## Tips

- Start with a clear biological question or unmet medical need
- Use the course notebooks as templates for your analysis
- Critical discussion of limitations is valued over perfect results
- Cite your sources and methods properly
