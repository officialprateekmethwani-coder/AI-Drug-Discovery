# Day 1: Introduction to AI in Drug Discovery

**Date:** 15 April 2026  
**Duration:** 6 Teaching Units

## Topics Covered

1. **The Drug Discovery Pipeline**
   - Target identification & validation
   - Hit finding & lead optimization
   - Preclinical & clinical development
   - Timelines, costs, and attrition rates

2. **Introduction to Artificial Intelligence**
   - Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning vs. AI
   - Supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning
   - Training, validation, and test sets
   - Overfitting, underfitting, and generalization

3. **AI Applications in Drug Discovery**
   - Virtual screening & molecular docking
   - QSAR modeling
   - De novo drug design
   - ADMET prediction
   - Protein structure prediction

4. **Molecular Representations for ML**
   - SMILES notation
   - Molecular fingerprints (ECFP/Morgan, MACCS)
   - Molecular descriptors
   - Graph representations

## Key Concepts

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| QSAR | Quantitative Structure-Activity Relationship — predicting biological activity from molecular structure |
| Fingerprint | Binary vector encoding molecular substructures |
| SMILES | Simplified Molecular Input Line Entry System — text representation of molecules |
| Descriptor | Numerical property calculated from a molecule (e.g., molecular weight, logP) |
| Virtual Screening | Computationally filtering large compound libraries to find potential drug candidates |
